The Ebbinghaus Science

Hermann Ebbinghaus discovered in 1885 that memory decays exponentially without reinforcement. The formula R = e^(−t/S) models this precisely. Your stability score S is calculated from your FPI — higher FPI means you forget faster. Spaced reviews increase S over time.
